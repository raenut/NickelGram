# NickelGram 0.1.2

## 修复与改进

- 切换书籍后立即分享，书名和作者会使用当前书籍的信息。
- 分享已保存高亮时，发送完整书摘和批注。
- Markdown 导出按书中顺序排列，并改进章节标题和段落格式。

## 已知限制

- 直接选区可能缺少原书的段落换行。
- 最终发布包未完成完整实机复测；其他设备和固件未验证。

# NickelGram 0.1.2

## Fixes and improvements

- Sharing after a book switch uses the current book's title and author.
- Sharing a saved highlight includes its full text and annotation.
- Markdown exports follow book order, with improved chapter headings and
  paragraph formatting.

## Known limits

- Direct selections may omit paragraph breaks.
- The final package did not receive a full device retest. Other devices and
  firmware remain unverified.
